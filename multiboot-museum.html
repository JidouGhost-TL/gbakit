GBA Single-Cartridge MultiBoot (NetBoot / Download Play) Architecture: Under the BIOS SWI 0x25 specification, a single master cartridge transmits a complete, self-contained ARM/Thumb program over serial link cable (SIO) into the 256 KiB EWRAM (0x02000000..0x0203FFFF) of up to three slave consoles. Client GBA consoles boot without a cartridge inserted, validate the 192-byte header (fixed 0x96 byte at +0xB2 and one's complement checksum at +0xBD), and jump directly to the ARM branch opcode at 0x020000C0 (typically vectoring into 0x0200010C).

GBA MultiBoot Protocol Specification & gbakit Integration

BIOS SWI 0x25 Calling Convention: In ARM mode: swi 0x250000 / In Thumb mode: swi 0x25 (Opcode 0xDF25). Register r0 points to a MultiBootParam structure residing in WRAM. The BIOS initiates a synchronous 115,200 baud Multi-Play packet handshake, streams 16-bit payload words to connected client slots, computes cryptographic CRC verification tokens, and transfers control to the client entry point. 
// gbakit CLI MultiBoot Inspection & Extraction
# Inspect embedded MultiBoot binaries inside any commercial GBA ROM:
gbakit rom multiboot <rom.gba> --json

# Extract standalone MultiBoot binary payload by index:
gbakit rom multiboot <rom.gba> -o work/payload_0.mb.gba --index 0 --json

# Verify payload entry point, EWRAM branch opcode, and complement checksum:
gbakit info work/payload_0.mb.gba --json
